Louis Thayer Hills

1935 – 2019

Louis Thayer Hills was a prolific Wisconsin artist whose work spanned more than six decades, creating abstract paintings and prints inspired by the landscapes and natural beauty of his beloved home state. Beginning in the 1950s, he produced a substantial and wide-ranging body of drawings, sketches, sculptures, paintings, and prints deeply rooted in southeastern Wisconsin. Hills' abstract artistic evolution mirrored the essence of Wisconsin itself—from the dark, brooding tones inspired by winter forests in his early oil paintings to the explosive colors drawn from prairie wildflowers and autumn foliage in his later acrylics. His mixed media works often incorporated natural materials and textures found in the Wisconsin countryside, translating the spirit of the land into bold, abstract expressions. Working from his home in southeastern Wisconsin, which he shared with his wife Kay, Hills drew continuous inspiration from the rolling farmlands, woodlands, and rural character that defined the region. His abstract compositions distilled the energy and color of Wisconsin's natural landscape into dynamic visual expressions, creating a unique artistic legacy as a Wisconsin abstract artist. This site honors his artistic legacy and shares information about an upcoming memorial exhibition and art sale, offering collectors the rare chance to acquire original works directly from the estate, with pricing structured to place the work into many hands.

About the Collection

The collection encompasses decades of abstract work across a broad range of media, subjects, and scales. From hundreds of pages of ink and marker in spiral-bound notebooks, to oversize masonite paintings 4' x 5', to stacks and stacks of etchings/prints on paper, the offerings range from intimate sketch studies to huge statement pieces.

Trade & Hospitality Viewing

Due to the scale of the collection, a dedicated viewing period will be offered for restaurants, hotels, interior designers, and other commercial buyers interested in acquiring multiple works.

This trade preview allows buyers to select cohesive groupings of original artworks and explore a wide range of sizes and styles well-suited for larger spaces. Works are available directly from the artist's estate, with pricing structured to support volume acquisitions.

Pricing & Acquisition

Pricing has been structured to reflect the size and breadth of the collection, with the intention of making original works accessible while respecting the artist's lifetime of production.
